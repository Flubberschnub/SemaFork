Terms of use

Semafork is a group dining decision tool. Use it to suggest places and vote with your group. Do not impersonate participants, share other people's access credentials, abuse the service, or submit unlawful content.

Restaurant information

Google Maps supplies restaurant information where available. Ratings, review excerpts, photographs and price levels may be missing or change, and are not guarantees about a restaurant. Confirm the location, hours, prices and suitability with the restaurant before visiting. Manual suggestions are supplied by participants, not verified by Google.

Google features

By using the Google Maps features, you are also bound by the Google Terms of Service and Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service. Google's content remains subject to those terms. Do not scrape or republish the restaurant content supplied through this app.

Availability and sessions

This version provides one vote per participant and allows the host to finish with the votes received. Keep your anonymous browser session to retain access to your identity. There is no account-based recovery. The service may be unavailable or changed by its operator.

Read the privacy page for information about party records, local storage and Google requests. Contact the operator who shared this installation for support concerning that installation.
