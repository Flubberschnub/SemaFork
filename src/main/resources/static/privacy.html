Privacy

Semafork lets a group suggest restaurants and vote without creating accounts. Use a nickname rather than personal information you do not want other participants to see.

Party information

The application stores party names, participant names, manual suggestions, selected Google Place IDs, votes and anonymous access tokens in its database. Party information is visible to people who access the party. Do not use party names or suggestions for sensitive information. This version does not automatically delete old parties; retention and deletion are managed by the deployment operator.

Browser storage

Your host and participant credentials are stored in this browser's local storage so you can return to your party. “Forget this device” or clearing site data removes these local credentials, not the server's party records. Without the credentials, your identity and host controls cannot be recovered by name alone.

Google Maps

When Maps is configured, opening a party loads Google Maps and sends browser/network information and place searches to Google. Restaurant details, reviews and photos are fetched directly from Google for display. Semafork persists the selected Place ID, not Google's review text, photographs, ratings, addresses or coordinates. Review authors and photo contributors may have public profile links.

“Use my location” asks for your browser's permission. The position is used in this page to center the map and bias searches sent to Google. Semafork does not save the position to its database or local storage. You can decline and search with a city name instead.

Google processes these interactions under the Google Privacy Policy, which applies to the Google features of this service. See also Google Maps terms.

Hosting and questions

The hosting operator may collect ordinary server access and error logs, including network information. Contact the operator who provided this Semafork URL for their hosting practices and requests concerning stored party data. Software issues can be reported in the project issue tracker; do not post tokens or private party information there.
